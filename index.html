Privacy Policy — Dyebrook

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Dyebrook is a puzzle game published by greybits. This policy explains what the game keeps, what it shares, and who to contact about it. It is written to be accurate rather than reassuring: where data does leave your device, it says so.

Contact: greysonmiller67@gmail.com

The short version

  • Dyebrook has no account, no login and no sign-up.
  • Your progress and settings never leave your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects data to serve them.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by the app store you installed the game from. We never see your payment details.
  • There is no analytics. We do not sell your data, and we do not build a profile of you.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the game is stored locally, in the game's own storage:

  • the pictures you have painted, with your stars and moves, and any picture you left half done
  • your daily and weekly pictures and your daily streak
  • your gallery: the frames you have earned, the frames you opened by watching a video, and which frame hangs on which painting
  • a note of which purchases you own, so the game can unlock them without asking the store every time
  • your settings: sound effects, music, vibration, theme, relaxed mode, colour symbols and the daily reminder
  • how many times the game has been opened today, used to decide when the ad on opening appears

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no server holding your game, and there is no way for us to recover it once it is gone.

Erasing it yourself

Settings → Your data has two choices. Each asks you to confirm first.

  • Reset progress starts every album again: your painted pictures, stars, half-done pictures, daily and weekly pictures, streak and the frames hung on your paintings are cleared. Your settings, your purchases and the frames you opened by watching a video stay.
  • Delete my data removes everything listed above and returns the game to the state of a new install. Items you paid for come back with Restore purchases in the Shop, because the store keeps them on your account; frames opened by watching a video do not come back.

Neither choice touches your advertising ID or your answer about ads. Those are kept by your device and by Google's consent form, not by the game: change them in your device's settings and in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Uninstalling the game also deletes all of it.

The daily reminder

The reminder is off until you turn it on (Daily → Remind me at 7 pm). When it is on, the game plans one notification on your device for 7 pm, only on days when the daily picture is not painted yet. Your device may ask whether the game may show notifications. The notification is planned and shown by your device alone: no server is involved, and nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Advertising

Dyebrook shows ads supplied by Google AdMob:

  • a small banner at the end of the menus, and under the picture while you play
  • a full-screen ad between pictures, after every second picture you paint
  • a full-screen ad when you open the game, from the third time you open it in a day
  • short videos you can choose to watch for three more hints, one more direct paint, or to open a gallery frame early

To serve those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your advertising ID
  • your IP address and an approximate, country-level location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, app version and language, and diagnostic information about how the ad software is running
  • how you interact with an ad: that it was shown, and whether you tapped it

Dyebrook asks for no location permission and never reads your GPS.

Google acts as an independent controller of that data. What it does with it is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Remove ads

Remove ads, a one-time purchase, takes away the banners and every full-screen ad. The videos you choose to watch stay available, so Google's ad software keeps running and may still collect the data above when it loads a video for you to choose.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Before any ad is requested, Dyebrook shows Google's consent form and asks whether ads may use your data. Nothing is requested until you have answered. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices, which opens the same form again.

Purchases

Dyebrook offers optional one-time purchases: Remove ads, the Direct paint pack (every picture starts with three direct paints instead of one) and both together. Gallery frames are never sold.

Purchases are processed entirely by the app store you installed the game from. The store tells the game which items you own so it can unlock them. We never see or receive your name, card number, billing address or any other payment detail: the game only ever learns that an item is owned.

Your purchases are tied to your store account, so reinstalling the game and tapping Restore purchases brings them back.

Analytics

Dyebrook contains no analytics software. We do not track sessions, screens or events, and we receive no reports about how you play.

Children

Dyebrook is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact greysonmiller67@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Data we hold about you

None. We operate no servers and have no database. Because the game has no account, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete: Settings → Delete my data, or deleting the game, removes your data completely.

Requests about data held by Google (advertising) or by the app store (purchases) must be made to those companies, through the links above and your store account settings, because we have no access to it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data about you, and to object to it being used for advertising. Since we hold no personal data ourselves, those rights are exercised:

  • For advertising: through the consent form in the game (Settings → Ad privacy choices) and through your device's settings, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • For purchases: through your app store account.
  • For everything stored in the game: with Settings → Delete my data (or Reset progress, for your progress only), or by deleting the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top changes with it, and the new version appears both here and inside the game under Settings → Privacy policy.
